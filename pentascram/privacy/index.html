PentaScram Privacy Policy

Published by Rats Games
Effective date: 14 September 2026

PentaScram is a word puzzle game published by Rats Games ("we", "us" or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PentaScram Android application.

Information stored by PentaScram

PentaScram does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address or other contact information to play the game.

Game information such as puzzle progress, game results, statistics, settings and notification preferences is stored locally on your device. This information is used to provide features including Daily puzzle progress, statistics and preferences.

We do not operate a PentaScram server that receives or stores your game history or personal account information.

Advertising

PentaScram displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • Device and advertising identifiers;
  • Information about interactions with the app and advertisements; and
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

Google may use this information for purposes including providing and measuring advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

For more information about how Google handles information, please see Google's Privacy Policy .

Purchases

PentaScram offers an optional one-time purchase to remove advertising. Purchases are processed by Google Play.

We do not receive or store your credit card, bank account or other payment credentials. Google processes the transaction according to its own terms and privacy policies.

PentaScram may locally store whether the Remove Ads purchase has been recognised so that the appropriate app experience can be provided.

Notifications

PentaScram can optionally send a local notification reminding you that a new Daily puzzle is available. You can choose whether to enable these notifications and can disable them through PentaScram's settings or your device settings.

Notification preferences are stored locally on your device.

Information sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

PentaScram uses third-party services supplied by Google, including Google AdMob and Google Play. These services may collect and process information as described above and according to their own privacy policies.

Data security

Game progress, statistics and PentaScram preferences are primarily stored locally on your device. Information transmitted by Google's services is handled according to Google's security practices. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Data retention and deletion

PentaScram's locally stored game information and preferences remain on your device unless removed through applicable app functionality, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the application.

Information collected independently by Google through Google AdMob or Google Play is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion policies.

Because PentaScram does not require or maintain a user account on our servers, there is no PentaScram online user account to delete.

Children's privacy

PentaScram does not knowingly ask users to provide personal information such as their name, email address, telephone number or home address.

Third-party advertising and services used by the app may process data as described in this Privacy Policy. The availability and treatment of advertising may also depend on the user's location, age, applicable laws and Google policies.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when PentaScram's features or third-party services change. Any revised policy will be published at this location with an updated effective date.
